# Borozdov Cirrus

A theme from the Borozdov collection. Two faces — light **Zenith**, a soft daylight notebook
on a pale sky, and dark **Nimbus**, the same notebook under a night cloud. Bone-white cards
with 32px corners, black pill buttons and one iris-blue stroke.

![Borozdov Cirrus in light mode](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/borozdov-obsidian-themes/cirrus/main/screenshots/light.png)

![Borozdov Cirrus in dark mode](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/borozdov-obsidian-themes/cirrus/main/screenshots/dark.png)

## Principles

- **Sky and bone.** The page is a pale sky and cards are bone white; the shift from one to
  the other is the depth, not a shadow.
- **Mid-weight, never bold.** Noto Sans Medium for the title and headings, tracked in as
  they grow; the platform's own sans for the text.
- **Black fills, one iris stroke.** Everything you fill is near-black by day and bone by
  night; iris blue is a stroke, for links, tags and the open file's outline.
- **Nothing is sharp.** 32px on panels, 24px on callouts, 16px on code, tables and fields,
  pills for everything you press.

## Features

- Light and dark modes, following Settings → Appearance → Base color scheme
- Callouts as bone-white cards whose icon sits in a pastel circle: lavender, mint, powder,
  sun or peach by type
- Tags as pills outlined in iris; the open file as a pill drawn with an iris stroke
- A sunny highlighter
- Quiet editing: no focus ring around the note, its title or form fields while you type;
  property names read as labels, not boxed fields
- Text colours meet WCAG contrast on both faces
- The phone layout keeps the same colours and shapes
- No `!important`: every rule can be overridden with a CSS snippet

## Installation

**From the community directory:** Settings → Appearance → Themes → Manage, search for
**Borozdov Cirrus**, then **Install and use**.

**By hand:** download `manifest.json` and `theme.css` from the [latest
release](https://github.com/borozdov-obsidian-themes/cirrus/releases/latest) into
`<vault>/.obsidian/themes/Borozdov Cirrus/`, then choose Borozdov Cirrus under Settings →
Appearance → Themes.

## Font

Cyrillic, for the title and headings only.
